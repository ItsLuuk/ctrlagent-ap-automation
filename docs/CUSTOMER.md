# Customer

## Job Statement

When invoices arrive, I want to turn them into trustworthy payable records automatically, so my team can move quickly without missing an invoice or approving fraud.

## Job Dimensions

### Functional

Reliably extract, reconcile, approve, and hand off every invoice.

**Underdelivery:** The approval-to-handoff boundary is the primary leak. The system must make omissions, mismatches, and blocked records visible rather than silently deferring them.

### Emotional

Feel calm and confident that routine invoices are handled and exceptions are visible.

**Underdelivery:** Interaction feedback is the roughest experience area. The system must confirm actions quickly and make system state understandable.

### Social

Produce a shared, traceable record that finance, owners, and auditors can defend.

**Underdelivery:** The handoff needs a clearer, trustworthy record of what was approved, what remains blocked, and what action is next.

## Hire Types

### Big Hire

Establish trust that the system can safely handle the first invoices.

### Little Hire

Repeatably review, approve, and hand off invoices with low cognitive load and no missed work.

### Current priority

Both matter, with the first evidence pass focused on the recurring approval-to-handoff boundary.

## Competing Alternatives

| Alternative | Why it is hired | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Manual keying | Familiar, immediate control over each invoice | Slow, repetitive, and vulnerable to omissions and transcription errors |

The remaining alternatives should be added after customer evidence or a deeper product audit.
